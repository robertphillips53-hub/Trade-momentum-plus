Scorecard Key

Composite is the overall Trade Momentum+ setup score after catalyst, momentum, entry structure and risk adjustments. Catalyst measures freshness, credibility and potential impact. Momentum considers RVOL, turnover/rotation, trend, price strength and liquidity. Entry asks whether the trade is attractive right now: breakout confirmation, room to next resistance, risk/reward, extension, support/reclaim quality, spread/liquidity and volume confirmation.

Trade Map: Trigger is the level/zone that must clear and hold. Next Resistance is the next meaningful overhead area. Room to Run measures available space when defensible. Invalidation identifies the level whose failure breaks the setup. “TBD next scan” means the system will not invent false precision.

Trade Momentum+ is a research dashboard, not a guarantee of performance.
