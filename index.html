Event-Repricing Scorecard

Evidence Confidence asks how strongly public evidence supports a favorable catalyst outcome. Repricing Potential asks how explosive the move could be if the thesis is right, emphasizing small companies and events that materially change company value. Pre-Catalyst measures whether risk/reward justifies positioning before the event. Post-Catalyst measures whether waiting for results and trading confirmation is preferable. These are separate decisions: a huge catalyst can have weak pre-event confidence, while a strong result can become an excellent post-event momentum setup.

Decision State: DISCOVERY → PRE-CATALYST RESEARCH → PRE-CATALYST ENTRY or WAIT FOR RESULTS → POST-CATALYST ENTRY or PASS → OUTCOME/LEARNING. Biotech and government/defense awards receive strong discovery attention, but every sector must pass evidence, novelty, economic materiality, priced-in, float/liquidity, dilution and downside checks.

Trade Map: Trigger is the level/zone that must clear and hold. Invalidation identifies what breaks the setup. “TBD” means the system will not invent false precision.
